Mining rewards are paid in Bitcoin, so a higher price lifts the dollar value of every sat while the electricity and service fees (quoted in dollars) stay fixed. At $100,000, the 100 TH hashrate nets about $93/month, and the $839 of GMT you lock for the 20% discount adds roughly $15/month in staking — about $109 combined on $2,471 of committed capital, that is 52.7% a year on capital at today's price — a strong rate for cloud mining, though it assumes you hold the maximum fee discount.

The reason a higher Bitcoin price helps so much: your fee is a dollar amount, so as price rises it shrinks as a share of your reward. That's the leverage — and the risk works in reverse if price falls. The locked GMT, meanwhile, is retained capital that keeps paying staking regardless of BTC.

Frequently asked questions

Is GoMining profitable at $100k Bitcoin?

At a $100,000 price and current difficulty, a 100 TH GoMining farm nets about $93/month from mining plus $15 from staking the GMT locked for the 20% discount — around $109 combined on $2,471 of capital. Earnings scale with your hashrate.

Why does the Bitcoin price matter so much for GoMining?

Rewards are paid in Bitcoin but fees are charged in dollars, so a higher BTC price raises your revenue while your cost stays fixed — every dollar of price increase lands in your margin. A falling price does the opposite.

Does difficulty change these numbers?

Yes, and this page holds it at today's level rather than guessing where it goes. Rising difficulty steadily reduces the sats each TH earns, so a figure computed at today's difficulty is a snapshot, not a forecast.
