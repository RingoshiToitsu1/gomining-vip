Two things get bought here, and most calculators only count the first. You mint 250 TH for about $4,019, and to hold the maximum 20% fee discount you must lock roughly $2,097 of GMT (360 days of fee coverage) — $6,116 of capital committed in total. On the income side, the hashrate nets about $95/month after fees and the 2.25% conversion, and the locked GMT earns roughly $38/month staking at 21.73% APR — about $133 combined, that is 26.1% a year on capital at today's price — a strong rate for cloud mining, though it assumes you hold the maximum fee discount.

Why the locked GMT is not a normal cost

Unlike the hashrate, the $2,097 in GMT isn't spent — you still own the tokens and can unlock them later, so it's capital tied up rather than money gone (with GMT price risk while it's locked). It also pulls double duty: it cuts your fee by 20% and earns 21.73% staking. Without any discount, 250 TH nets only about $61/month and ties up no GMT — but you leave the fee saving and the staking on the table. That trade is the main lever you control.

Why there is no multi-year forecast on this page

Every figure above is today's: live Bitcoin price ($62,901), live network difficulty, current fee rates and the 21.73% staking APR. None of it is projected forward, because a multi-year total printed into a static page is a number you did not choose and cannot inspect — and it will be read as a promise no matter how it is labelled.
