Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 Nov 2026

GMT-Optimizer ("we", "us") is a free, independent set of GoMining calculators at gmt-optimizer.com, run by Ringo. This policy explains what we store, why, who processes it, and how to get it deleted. We collect as little as we can.

Using the site without an account

Every calculator works without an account. We run no analytics and no ads, and set no tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps your own settings on your device (for example the farm you entered, display currency, language choice and whether you've dismissed a tip). That data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Accounts

An account is optional. It needs only a username and a password; we never ask for an email address. Passwords are stored hashed by our database provider, Supabase; we can't read them. Because there is no email, a forgotten password is reset by hand: message @RingoMining on Telegram.

If you create an account, we store:

  • Profile: username, optional display name, bio and profile picture, the date you joined, and your role (user or moderator).
  • Saved farm: the calculator inputs you save (hashrate, efficiency, GMT and related settings) and your total hashrate.
  • Miners: the miners you add to your fleet (collection, number, hashrate, efficiency).
  • Language preference, if you set one in Settings.

What others can see: your username, display name, picture, bio, join date, role and total hashrate appear on your public profile and in chat. You can hide your hashrate in Settings. Your saved farm and miners are visible only to you.

Chat

Messages in the community chat are public to anyone visiting the site, with your username and picture. Messages are deleted automatically after 24 hours. Moderators can delete messages and ban accounts that break the rules.

Profile pictures

Pictures you upload are stored in Supabase storage and are publicly viewable by link. Removing your picture in Settings deletes the file.

The co-pilot's "send Ringo my username" form

If you choose to send your details through the co-pilot, we collect your name or handle, your GoMining username, the contact you give (email, Telegram or X), and the page you sent it from. We use it only to confirm you signed up with code RINGO5 and to help you set up your farm. The form is processed and stored by Formspree (Formspree Privacy Policy). We keep it while we're helping you and delete it afterwards, or sooner on request.

Who processes data

  • Supabase: accounts, saved farms, miners, chat and profile pictures.
  • Formspree: the co-pilot form.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the site and may log technical data such as IP addresses.
  • Market data and fonts: your browser fetches live prices from CoinPaprika, CoinGecko and mempool.space, scripts from jsDelivr and fonts from Google Fonts. These services see your IP address when your browser contacts them.

We don't sell or share your personal data for marketing.

Keeping and deleting your data

Account data is kept until you delete your account. To delete it, go to Settings → Delete account: this removes your profile, saved farm, miners, profile picture and chat messages. Chat messages are deleted after 24 hours in any case. To have co-pilot form data deleted, message us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK/EU GDPR and similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its processing, and to data portability. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the data we hold, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure.

Children

GMT-Optimizer is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes are reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

For any privacy question or data request, message @RingoMining on Telegram.
