How GoMining ROI works

Return on investment is simply your net earnings over what you put in. For GoMining, net earnings come from daily mining rewards minus your electricity fee:

daily net = (sats/TH/day × your TH × BTC price) − (electricity fee × (1 − GMT discount))

What matters is what the setup pays you now, measured against everything it ties up — hashrate and locked GMT both.

The variables that actually move it

  • Bitcoin price — sets the dollar value of every sat you mine.
  • Network difficulty — rises over time, steadily reducing sats earned per TH. Ignoring this is why flat calculators mislead.
  • Miner efficiency (W/TH) — lower watts-per-TH means a smaller electricity fee for the same power. The biggest long-term lever.
  • GMT discount — locking GMT cuts your electricity fee in steps up to 20%, directly improving net earnings.

Why a projection beats a straight-line estimate

A realistic model has to erode rewards over time — through Bitcoin halvings and the steady grind of rising difficulty — otherwise it dramatically overstates your returns. Frequently asked questions

How is GoMining ROI calculated?

GoMining ROI is your net earnings measured against what you invested. Net earnings are daily mining rewards (sats per TH per day) minus your electricity fee after any GMT discount, plus staking on the GMT you lock. A good calculator projects that forward through halvings and rising difficulty to show what you actually receive each year.

What is the break-even point in GoMining?

This calculator does not give you a break-even date. A payback period tells you when you stop being down rather than what you receive, and pinning it to a date requires guessing the Bitcoin price years ahead. It shows what your setup earns today, per day and per month, against the real capital it ties up. To model what happens next, use the projection tool and set the assumptions yourself.

What affects GoMining profitability the most?

Three variables dominate: the Bitcoin price, network difficulty, and your miner's efficiency in watts per TH. The GMT discount on your electricity fee is the main lever you directly control.
