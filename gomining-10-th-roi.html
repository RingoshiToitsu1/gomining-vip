Why the locked GMT is not a normal cost

Unlike the hashrate, the $84 in GMT isn't spent — you still own the tokens and can unlock them later, so it's capital tied up rather than money gone (with GMT price risk while it's locked). It also pulls double duty: it cuts your fee by 20% and earns 21.73% staking. Without any discount, 10 TH nets only about $3/month and ties up no GMT — but you leave the fee saving and the staking on the table. That trade is the main lever you control.
