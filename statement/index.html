Mining income statement

Drop in the income CSV that GoMining exports for an account and this builds a dated statement from it — headline figures, four charts and a month-by-month summary. Print it to PDF and send it on. Every number traces back to a row in the file; nothing here is projected or estimated.

📊
Drop the GoMining CSV here, or click to choose
The file is named something like 2026-01-01-2026-09-10_nft_incomes.csv
Export it from GoMining under Rewards → History → Download CSV.
🔒The file never leaves this device. It is read and charted in your browser — nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere, and closing the tab discards it.
Leave at 100% for the whole farm. Set a share to split one owner’s slice out of a jointly-funded fleet — income, costs and hashrate are stated pro rata, and the share is printed on the statement.
Figures are read from the GoMining export only and cover mining income; staking rewards on locked GMT are not included. US dollar amounts are converted at each day’s recorded BTC price, so they reflect value when earned rather than today’s market value. Unaudited — for information only, not financial advice.
