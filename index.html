FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is GoMining profitable?

It depends on the Bitcoin price, network difficulty and your miner's efficiency (watts per TH). GMT-Optimizer works all three out with live data, so you can see what a setup earns before you invest, including the downside.

How does the GMT discount work?

Paying maintenance in GMT gets a discount based on how many days of fees your GMT covers: every 18 days of coverage is 1% off, up to 20% at 360 days. Locked GMT also counts toward VIP tiers. The capital planner works out how much to lock.

How is GoMining ROI calculated?

The calculator takes daily mining rewards (sats per TH per day) from live network data, subtracts electricity and service fees after any discount, and projects the result through halvings and difficulty growth to show what you earn each year.

What do I get with code RINGO5?

Three sign-up bonuses from GoMining: +5% bonus TH on your first miner (up to 25 TH), 1 month of VIP Platinum+, and a Card cashback bonus when you spend with the GoMining Card in your first 90 days. Enter RINGO5 before your first purchase, within 30 days of signing up. New accounts only. Bonuses are set by GoMining and may change.

Are the numbers guaranteed?

No. Every figure is an estimate at today's rates. Rewards move with the Bitcoin price and network difficulty, and GoMining can change its prices and fees. Nothing on this site is financial advice.

Is GMT-Optimizer affiliated with GoMining?

No. GMT-Optimizer is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by GoMining.
