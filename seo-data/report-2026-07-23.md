## What moved

No week-over-week comparison is possible. Within the window, one trend clears the thresholds:

| Period | Impressions | Clicks | CTR | Imp/day |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jun 26 – Jul 15 | 117 | 19 | 16.2% | 5.9 |
| Jul 16 – 23 | 197 | 3 | 1.5% | 24.6 |

197 impressions is over the 100-impression bar, so the CTR collapse is a real pattern and not one-click noise. The reading: the early window's clicks were brand traffic on a handful of impressions; the late window's volume is non-brand queries arriving at positions 7–15, where almost nothing gets clicked. Impressions before Jun 30 were zero — the site was effectively invisible three weeks ago.

Too early to attribute to any recent site change. Google needs 2–8 weeks to recrawl and re-rank, so anything shipped this fortnight is not the cause of the Jul 16 ramp.

**Not** clearing the bar: no single query reached 30 impressions all window (the largest is "is gomining worth it" at 16). Query-level ranking work is currently unmeasurable.

## Worth acting on

1. **Three pages are parked at the bottom of page 1 and that is the whole click problem.** `gomining-roi-calculator.html` (97 imp, 2 clicks, pos 9.02), `gomining-discount-explained.html` (90 imp, 1 click, pos 9.12), `is-gomining-worth-it.html` (42 imp, 0 clicks, pos 8.48) — together 229 impressions and 3 clicks, 1.3% CTR. At position ~9 that CTR is roughly what the position earns, so this is a ranking constraint, not a snippet or title problem. Rewriting meta descriptions here would be wasted work. The lever is equity: the homepage sits at 4.34 with 20.7% CTR and holds the site's ranking strength — add prominent internal links from it to these three with exact-match anchors ("GoMining ROI calculator", "is GoMining worth it"). Moving 9 → 5 is worth roughly 3× the clicks on the same impressions.
2. **`gomining-promo-code.html` is invisible at position 30.9 — stop feeding it.** 36 impressions, 1 click, and it is absorbing 11 distinct promo/coupon/code variants that rank between 25 and 54 ("gomining promo code" 37.3, "gomining promo codes" 32.3, "gomining coupon" 34, "gomining code parrainage" 43). Eleven near-duplicate variants all stuck on page 3–4 is an authority problem, not a coverage gap — more promo-keyword pages will land in the same place. Recommend no further investment in this cluster until item 1 has moved; it is worth tens of clicks at best even if fully won.
3. **Non-brand clicks are ~4 in 28 days, and the data now says this plainly.** 12 of 22 clicks come from one navigational query ("gmt optimizer", pos 1, 80% CTR) and 18 of 22 land on the homepage. The branded `gomining-*` cluster is close to fully harvested at this size. Real growth needs non-branded intent — "cloud mining worth it", "bitcoin mining without hardware" — and there is currently zero impression data on those because no page targets them. That is the gap to plan against, not a number to keep re-reporting.

## What's working

The homepage: 20.7% CTR at position 4.34, above the ~7–9% typical for that position, though brand navigational demand inflates it. It also carries every ranking-equity signal on the site — which is what makes it the right source for the internal links in item 1.
