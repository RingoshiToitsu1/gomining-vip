## Hypotheses tested this run

Three candidate technical causes were checked against the actual site and
**ruled out**:

1. **Extensionless vs `.html` duplicate URLs.** GSC reports indexed pages as
   `/gomining-roi-calculator.html` while the homepage links
   `/gomining-roi-calculator`. Checked: every key page carries a correct
   `<link rel="canonical">` to the extensionless form
   (`index.html:9`, `gomining-roi-calculator.html:8`, `is-gomining-worth-it.html`,
   `gomining-discount-explained.html`, `gomining-promo-code.html`,
   `how-gomining-works.html`, and all the `*-th-roi` / `*-btc-*` pages). Not a
   defect. No action.
2. **`/gmt` vs `/gmt/` split** (separate `by_page` rows, 5 and 1 impressions).
   `gmt/index.html:8` canonicalises to `https://gmt-optimizer.com/gmt`. Already
   correct, and both rows are far below the 20-impression floor anyway. No action.
3. **"The homepage doesn't link the three page-9 guides."** False — it does, at
   `index.html:384–389` plus the footer at `413`. An initial grep for `.html`
   hrefs missed them because the links are extensionless. The prior run's
   recommendation to "add internal links" was therefore already partly satisfied;
   what remains is *anchor text* and *prominence*, which is what today's action
   narrows to.

## Noted, below the action bar

- **Homepage/ROI-page cannibalisation on "gomining calculator".** The homepage
  H2 (`index.html:373`) is "The GoMining calculator, explained." and the homepage
  ranks 23.5 for that query while `/gomining-roi-calculator.html` ranks 15.75 for
  it — two pages competing, neither winning. Real, but 10 impressions total is
  below the bar. Backlogged, not acted on.
- **`gomining-worth-it-now.html` canonicalises to `/is-gomining-worth-it`**
  (`gomining-worth-it-now.html:8`). It will therefore never rank independently
  despite sitting at position 3.14 on 7 impressions. Plausibly deliberate
  (a live-figures variant deduped to the evergreen page) but worth a decision.
- **`/gomining-promo-code.html` at position 30.86** absorbing 11 near-duplicate
  promo/coupon/code variants ranking 25–54. Eleven variants all stuck on pages
  3–4 is an authority problem, not a coverage gap; more promo pages would land in
  the same place. Worth tens of clicks at best. No investment until the page-9
  cluster moves.
- **`terms.html` (26 imp, pos 2.65) and `privacy.html` (15 imp, pos 5.33)** rank
  well on brand queries and take 0 clicks. Harmless; they are absorbing
  "gmt optimizer" impressions that the homepage already wins at position 1.
- **Non-brand clicks are ~4 in 28 days.** 12 of 22 clicks come from one
  navigational query ("gmt optimizer", pos 1, 80% CTR) and 18 of 22 land on the
  homepage. The branded `gomining-*` cluster is near fully harvested at this
  size; real growth needs non-branded intent ("cloud mining worth it",
  "bitcoin mining without hardware"), for which there is currently zero
  impression data because no page targets it. Strategic gap, not a daily action.
