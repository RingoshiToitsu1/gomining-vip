About GMT-Optimizer

GMT-Optimizer is an independent toolkit for GoMining users: a live calculator, a capital planner and growth projections, built so you can see what a setup earns before you put money in.

Who builds it

I'm Ringo. I mine on GoMining with my own money, and I built GMT-Optimizer because I wanted honest numbers: live network data, every fee counted, and projections that show the downside as well as the upside.

How it's paid for

GMT-Optimizer has no ads. GoMining pays me a referral commission plus ongoing ambassador rewards when someone signs up with my code, RINGO5. Donations from the footer help too. None of that changes the numbers the tools show. See the disclosures for the full picture.

Independent

GMT-Optimizer is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by GoMining. Everything here is an estimate, not financial advice.
