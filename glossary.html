GoMining glossary

The terms used across GMT-Optimizer and GoMining, in plain English.

Fees and rates as of 17 Nov 2026. They may change.

TH (terahash)
A unit of mining power: one trillion hashes per second. GoMining miners are sized in TH, and rewards scale with it.
W/TH (efficiency)
Watts of electricity per TH. Lower is better: a 12 W/TH miner pays less electricity fee than a 15 W/TH one for the same hashrate.
Efficiency upgrade
A one-time purchase that lowers a miner's W/TH, bought one step at a time. Steps get more expensive closer to 12 W/TH.
Maintenance fee
The daily cost of running a miner: an electricity fee (based on W/TH) plus a service fee, deducted from rewards.
GMT
GoMining's token. Paying maintenance in GMT unlocks the fee discount; locking it also counts toward VIP tiers and earns staking rewards.
Fee discount (coverage)
The discount on maintenance from holding enough GMT to cover your fees: 1% per 18 days of coverage, up to 20%.
Locked GMT (veGMT)
GMT locked for a period on GoMining. It counts toward coverage and VIP tier, and earns a staking yield.
VIP tier
A GoMining level reached by hashrate or locked GMT, whichever is higher. Higher tiers add a small extra fee discount.
Click streak
A small extra discount for logging in and confirming daily on GoMining, held once the streak is kept.
Solo mining
A GoMining mining mode with its own small fee discount. The calculator includes it when it applies.
Greedy Machine
A GoMining miner whose hashrate grows by a small percentage every week, for free. The marketplace checker sizes what one is worth.
Marketplace
Where GoMining users buy and sell existing miners, priced in GMT.
sats/TH/day
Bitcoin earned per TH per day, in satoshis (1 BTC = 100,000,000 sats). It falls as network difficulty rises.
Network difficulty
How hard it is to mine a Bitcoin block. It adjusts about every two weeks as total network hashrate changes.
Halving
The roughly four-yearly event that halves the Bitcoin block reward. The next is expected in 2028.
ROI
Return on investment: what a setup earns relative to what it costs. On this site, always an estimate at today's rates.
RINGO5
A GoMining referral code that unlocks three sign-up bonuses for new accounts. See RINGO5 bonuses.
