Two things get bought here, and most calculators only count the first. You mint 500 TH for about $8,894, and to hold the maximum 20% fee discount you must lock roughly $4,194 of GMT (360 days of fee coverage) — $13,088 of capital committed in total. On the income side, the hashrate nets about $196/month after fees and the 2.25% conversion, and the locked GMT earns roughly $76/month staking at 21.73% APR — about $272 combined, that is 24.9% a year on capital at today's price — a strong rate for cloud mining, though it assumes you hold the maximum fee discount and Bitcoin holds its price.

Why the locked GMT is not a normal cost

Unlike the hashrate, the $4,194 in GMT isn't spent — you still own the tokens and can unlock them later, so it's capital tied up rather than money gone (with GMT price risk while it's locked). It also pulls double duty: it cuts your fee by 20% and earns 21.73% staking. Without any discount, 500 TH nets only about $127/month and ties up no GMT — but you leave the fee saving and the staking on the table. That trade is the main lever you control.

What the projection assumes

The projection holds Bitcoin and GMT flat (BTC at $63,641), erodes mining rewards over time through halvings and rising network difficulty, and keeps staking income steady. That erosion is why the per-day figure falls across the table even as the total climbs.
