What is GMT-Optimizer?

An independent calculator, capital planner and growth projection for GoMining. It uses live Bitcoin price and network difficulty, so the figures reflect today's conditions. It is free to use and has no ads.

Is GMT-Optimizer an official GoMining site?

No. GMT-Optimizer is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by GoMining.

Is GoMining profitable?

It depends on the Bitcoin price, network difficulty and your miner's efficiency (watts per TH). The calculator works all three out with live data, so you can see what a setup earns before you invest, including the downside.

How does the GMT discount work?

Paying your maintenance in GMT gets a discount based on how many days of fees your GMT covers: every 18 days of coverage is 1% off, up to 20% at 360 days. Locked GMT also counts toward VIP tiers. The capital planner finds how much to lock.

How is GoMining ROI calculated?

The tool computes daily mining rewards (sats per TH per day) from live network data, subtracts your electricity and service fees after any discount, and projects the result through halvings and difficulty growth to show what you earn each year.

What do I get with code RINGO5?

Three sign-up bonuses from GoMining: +5% bonus TH on your first miner (up to 25 TH), 1 month of VIP Platinum+, and a Card cashback bonus when you spend with the GoMining Card in your first 90 days. Enter RINGO5 before your first purchase, within 30 days of signing up. New accounts only. Bonuses are set by GoMining and may change.

Are the projections guaranteed?

No. Every figure is an estimate. Rewards move with the Bitcoin price and network difficulty, and GoMining can change its prices and fees. Nothing on this site is financial advice.

Do I need an account?

No. Every tool works without logging in. An account saves your farm to the cloud so it follows you between devices, and lets you post in the community chat.

I forgot my password. What now?

Accounts use a username, not an email, so there is no reset email. Message Ringo on Telegram with your username and it will be reset by hand.

Where does the data come from?

Bitcoin price from CoinPaprika, CoinGecko and mempool.space; network difficulty from mempool.space; the GMT price from CoinPaprika and CoinGecko. TH and upgrade prices are read off the GoMining app and dated on the calculator.
